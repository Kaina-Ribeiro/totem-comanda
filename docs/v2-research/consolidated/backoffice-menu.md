# Backoffice menu — consolidated requirements

## Summary
- **Verdicts (145 features):** IN 98 · MODEL-ONLY 3 · CUT 11 · OUT 13 · OPEN 20 (each points to a question). HW-LATER 0 here.
- **Biggest gaps in v2:** no way to hide an item or to mark it sold out until a date (`ITEM` only has `available`); no visible state separate from stock; no sections per-channel hours or date windows; no promotion rules (`Discount` and `Award` are results only, not rules); no translations; no allergen "may contain" or "not informed" state; no tags, badges or cross-sell rule.
- **What Comanda already has:** the menu graph (sections, items, sized items, modifier groups, combos), section weekly hours, item prices and channel flags, modifier min/max and default quantity, combo ids, the fixed allergen list, and promotion code entry.
- **Silent-drop risk:** v2 drops broken links, empty groups, swap sells without items and loops with no error. Comanda must validate and reject before saving (V2 block). This is the single most important rule in this area.
- **Conflicts with the decisions:** the kiosk computes "from" prices in the front end (against "prices only from the Go API"). Q2 settles it.

Legend: IN = in scope · MODEL-ONLY = in the model, no screen now · CUT = brief rule 5 · OUT = decision 2, 8 or 9 · OPEN = question below · HW-LATER = hardware (none here).
