## Notes

### Tables, checks, waiters (the new features)

- The spec has no table, seat, check (bill), guest count, call-waiter or request-bill entity. The rows marked T cover what does exist.
- The only order type with a seat is `DINEIN` (ordering.md, scalar fields). Store hours have a `seat` flag (MODELS 9.10).
- `pay-waiter` is a payment provider that maps to a request-for-bill or waiter-call flow. It is never stored locally (payment-providers.md). This is the closest thing to pay-at-table in the spec.
- Waiter identity: the clerk lookup returns an empty object `{}` for the group POS (session-and-configuration.md, EP-SES-05). No waiter id, name or role exists in the spec, so the waiter model must be designed.
- The only open-check model is `IPosCheck` (MODELS 9.7: guestCount, employeeId, checkOpenedAt). The spec has no source for it. `openCheck` (MODELS 8.12) also has no spec equivalent: the spec places and pays orders at once.

### What the v2 models can express but the spec cannot

- Delivery: the spec has no delivery address, delivery charge or delivery order type. Only buzzer and name or phone (DM-ORD-08). Address and delivery charge come only from MODELS.
- Scheduled orders: `dateToFire` and `pickupTime` (MODELS 8.12) have no spec field.
- Per-order-type prices and flags, line taxes and discounts, `parentId` and `uniqueId` on option lines: the spec nests options by `options` or `modifiers` and computes totals only at the order level.
- Fiscal fields (`invoiceCpfCnpj`, NFC-e key): not in the BFF. Only in the receipt and print routes (phase 4).
- Menu fields such as `allergens`, `ageRestriction`, `alcoholic`, `calories`, `seatFlag` and `dineinFlag` are not in the spec's menu. They come from plan-next (G-A7, G-A8) and MODELS 5.9.
- Store hours: the spec uses day numbers (1 = Sunday to 7 = Saturday) with `HH:mm:ss.SSS`, MODELS section hours use day names with `HH:mm:ss`, and the spec gives two windows per day.

### Contradictions inside the spec, or between the spec and the plan

1. **Money unit.** Examples use integer units and GBP (ordering.md `total=1250`, menu-and-resources.md `price 999`, payments.md `monetaryAmount` in minor units, FR-ORD-008 "integer minor units"). MODELS.md uses decimal BRL, and CLAUDE.md uses integer cents in BRL. Decide the unit before the mock is extended.
2. **Close minute.** BR-SES-04 and DT-SES-01 count the close minute as open. DM-SES-01 says close is exclusive. plan-next says menu windows are end-exclusive and not to copy the reference.
3. **Payment states.** The `paymentState` list in payments.md (AUTH_ERROR, CAPTURE_ERROR, VOIDED, VOID_ERROR, SUCCESS) differs from DM-PAY-04 (REFUNDED, CANCEL_ERROR, no SUCCESS in the same role).
4. **PE04.** DM-PAY-02 calls PE04 "crypto-cancel". payments.md calls it "refund error", and EP-PAY-10 uses PE04 for crypto cancel.
5. **Authorised is success or not.** BR-PAY-03 counts AUTHORIZED as success. DM-PAY-04 maps AUTHORIZED to CAPTURE_PENDING. plan-next says authorised is not paid.
6. **Order failure after timeout.** BR-ORD-04 and FR-ORD-004 poll 3 times at 5 s, then report failure. plan-next says never report failed while the result is unknown.
7. **Silent drops.** BG-BKG-11, UJ-PAY-06 and FR-PAY-007 drop or ignore payments that cannot be saved. plan-next forbids this.
8. **Fail-open.** BG-BKG-07 treats an unreachable maintenance check as LIVE, and VAL-BKG keeps serving stale menus. plan-next says fail closed.
9. **Abandon.** EP-SES-04 also runs the cancel-partial-payments step (split bill, cut) and wraps every error into a 500.
10. **Gift card and other tenders outside rule 5.** Gift card, room charge, stored value (Heartland), food stamp, face-ID, crypto, app gateway and senior discount are not in brief rule 5, but plan-next cuts them. They are marked `?`. Tips and donations are in scope by rule 4, but CLAUDE.md lists tips as out of scope (the brief overrides CLAUDE.md here).
11. **Naming and numbering.** `guestName` (FR-SES-006) vs `name` (EP-SES-06). `imageId` (EP-MNU-06) vs `id` (validation doc). `/punchh/store` (loyalty overview) vs `/punchh/receipt` (route index). DM-SES-02, DM-SES-03 and DM-SES-04 are each used for two or more different bodies. FR-OPS-006 sits under the ORD heading. FR-ORD-011 to 013 sit under PAY. feature-checklist CHK-ORD-009 cites BR-ORD-09/10 for totals (they are BR-ORD-05/06).
12. **Example vs rule.** EP-SES-01 example shows state READY, while the rule says CACHE_NOT_READY is the not-ready state. EP-MNU-01 gives 500 on no cache, while the validation doc gives 200 success false.
