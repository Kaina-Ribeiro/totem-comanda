## Notes

### Contradictions inside the chunk

- CFG-663 is labelled both "Optional integer" and "Required" in the same entry.
- CFG-661 gives two maximum lengths, 256 and 100.
- CFG-653 and CFG-654 share the label "Choose collection slots:" but describe different things (recurrence structure and time).
- CFG-085 (delivery time, zone) has the effect text of CFG-660 (lead time). Copy error, probably.
- CFG-088 is "Minimum collection order" under a delivery zone, but FEAT-022 says "optional minimum basket".
- CFG-091 is labelled "Upper basket threshold", but FEAT-022 calls it the free-delivery threshold. CFG-070 also says "free delivery for orders above" with a vague effect.
- CFG-038 and CFG-039 (latitude and longitude) are numbers with a "max 100 characters" rule.
- CFG-068 reads "up to100" (missing space); the max is probably 100.
- CFG-059 has a doubled full stop in the choice text ("locked states..").
- CFG-049 and CFG-052 are both labelled "Enabled", for different features.
- FEAT-021 uses automatic save with a 1-second debounce. FEAT-015 uses explicit Save and Discard. Two save models on the same screen family.
- FEAT-015 says "county is cleared by the save path", but no county field is listed.
- Phone length differs: store phone CFG-044 max 100; service contact phone CFG-071 max 45.
- FEAT-076 pre-orders (decision 17 in plan-next says "not in the pilot") and FEAT-076 scheduled orders are IN by brief rule 4 ("scheduled orders"). Conflict to settle with Kai.
- Per-service store hours (CFG-097 to CFG-124) are IN by brief rule 1, but plan-next (4 Nov) says "Opening hours = the menu windows; there is no separate weekly store schedule". Conflict to settle with Kai.
- Delivery (CFG-066, 067, 068, 073, 084 to 092) is IN by brief rule 4 ("delivery with address and delivery charge"), but plan-next cuts "delivery and marketplaces". Conflict to settle with Kai.
- Per-service tax groups (CFG-083, CFG-090) are IN by brief rule 1 (TAX groups), but plan-next cuts "tax groups per service". Conflict to settle with Kai.
- "Peak" preparation time (CFG-058) is a different state from plan-next "Pausado".

### Unknowns the spec admits

- Overnight and overlap rules for opening periods (FEAT-024).
- Precedence of special dates over existing hours (FEAT-030; plan-next decision 6 proposes one).
- Backend geographic matching and zone overlap precedence (FEAT-022).
- Unit of "reject order after" (CFG-078 to CFG-080). No unit is given.
- Unit of the delivery-zone radius ("selected distance unit", CFG-086).
- Unit of delivery time (CFG-069, CFG-082, CFG-085).
- What "operational opening hours" are (CFG-125 to CFG-127).
- What "custom fields" and "parent location" mean (CFG-632, CFG-635).
- Meaning of "sync" in schedule frequency choices (CFG-651).
- Who receives outlet order routing (FEAT-075).
- Whether an "event store" is a store type (FEAT-021, FEAT-023).
- Names of the on-hold and cancelled internal states (store operational mode).

### What a v2 model cannot express (in the sections assigned)

- Dine-in and take-out hours: Hours (9.10) has only pickup, delivery, seat and opening flags.
- Dated exceptions (special dates, closed days, date-range hours): no v2 structure in the sections read.
- Order capacity limits and order intervals per service, and kitchen capacity: no v2 field.
- Store address lines 1 to 3, coordinates, cuisine tags, store description, lead times by state: StoreConfigurationResponse (9.10) has only locationName, email, phone, liveFlag, deliveryCharge and hours.
- Delivery zones (radius, geometry, zone fee, zone tax group, zone external ID): no v2 entity in the sections read.
- Delivery charge as a percentage: deliveryCharge is a plain number.
- Order to table: v2 has no OrderType for it. Closest is DINEIN with the seatFlag (5.19). ValidateTableRequest.tableNumber is a string, but the spec uses integer seat ranges (1 to 9999) and a free-text location option.
- Events, recurrence, collection-slot offsets and pre-order opening: no v2 entity. dateToFire and pickupTime on the order are the closest.
- Demo and offline store modes: liveFlag is 1 or 0 only.
- Item instruction required flag, default visibility and length limit: OrderItem.specialNotes has no such fields in the sections read.
- Hours day and time formats: 9.10 uses day numbers 1 to 7 and "HH:mm:ss.SSS"; 5.8 uses day names and "HH:mm:ss". The spec here uses HH:mm defaults (00:00, 23:59).

### Other notes

- No waiter, check, bill, guest count, service charge, call-waiter or pay-at-table line exists in this chunk. The only table or seat lines are the seat, location and seat-service rows marked T = Y.
- Dine-in rows (CFG-110 to CFG-114) are not marked T. The spec does not say dine-in is table based. Confirm with Kai before the check and table work starts.
- The Area column is where the owner edits the value. Guest-facing effects are named in the What column.
- The spec admits that store details and phone settings are not saved atomically (FEAT-015). plan-next and the Comanda rule on saveBoth already cover this pattern.
- Several rows are CUT only because they cover more than one store (organisation publish, store groups, bulk status). Their single-store equivalents, where they exist, are marked IN.
