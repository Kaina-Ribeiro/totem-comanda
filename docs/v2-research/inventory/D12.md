## Notes
- **Contradictions**
  1. Allergen policy mode is a Boolean (CFG-597, line 546), but the same choice for legal documents is an enum (CFG-566, line 81).
  2. CFG-604 to 606 sit under FEAT-071 (table-bill) but their acceptance text says "legacy location field". The spec also says the two custom-field entities are not merged. The owner of these three fields is unclear.
  3. CFG-717 (order message) has an Effect that reads "Hosted legal content", copied from the legal templates. Its context is the order message, so the Effect is probably a copy error.
  4. CFG-712 "Store banner" has the email form (FORM-097) as context, so its scope is unclear.
  5. Upload limits disagree: FEAT-064 says 20 MB for media, FEAT-081 says 2 MB for image fields, and the loyalty background says 4 MB.
  6. Duration: the UI shows seconds, the stored value is milliseconds (CFG-550). The contract must pick one unit.
- **Unknowns the spec admits**
  - Conflict winner for overlapping media (FEAT-064).
