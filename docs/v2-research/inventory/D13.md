## Notes

**Contradictions and unclear wording in the chunk**
- CFG-499 (hero schedule) has no verified new-record default, but FEAT-060 says new records start with schedule off.
- CFG-620 is typed Boolean but lists two named choices ("All customers / Specific customers only").
- CFG-022 is typed "Boolean/reference".
- CFG-033 ("Group image:") and FEAT-014 (image max 2 MB) do not match CFG-028 (no size limit). Same concept, two sizes.
- CFG-020 ("YY") says "Maximum: 99". This is a numeric maximum, not a length, for a two-digit year. Unclear.
- CFG-522 says positions 1, 2 and 8 are fixed; FEAT-062 says indices 0, 1 and 7. These are the same (0-based vs 1-based).
- Copy issues to keep out of Comanda: "next100" (CFG-670), "Save this settings" (CFG-675), double period (CFG-015).

**Money: a conflict with the current Comanda contract**
- The v2 model and this chunk use a decimal in BRL.
- `CLAUDE.md` says money is integer cents. Flag to Kai before any money field moves.

**Plan-next cut list vs brief rule 5**
- These rows are CUT because `docs/plan-next.md` cuts them (cut list or settled decision), not because brief rule 5 names them: guest conversion and customer accounts; customer name, email and phone (guest contact); marketing and consent opt-ins (CFG-009, CFG-671, FEAT-058 opt-out); SaaS billing (CFG-017 to CFG-020, FEAT-011); cash (FEAT-079); event pickup and busiest events (FEAT-077).
