### Boilerplate (read in full, recorded as pattern rows)
- **Pattern A, field rows** (FR-424 to FR-744): "X editable under stated access and mode", "With Y loaded and the stated access granted, editing X must produce the effect above", "Enforce its requiredness...". Identical in every row. Each row's own Setting name, type, limit, restriction and expected behaviour were recorded.
- **Pattern B, action rows** (FR-745 to FR-884): "Apply the specific permission, validation and confirmation rules; no generic undo" and "Perform X in the indicated context... without treating request acceptance as job completion". Each action is one row. The feature-level text (save rules, autosave, publish rules) is in What where it matters.
- Restriction text differs only in the rows listed under contradictions and unknowns below.

### Contradictions inside the chunk
- FR-661: collection-slot name carries two limits on one row, max 256 and max 100.
- FR-425 and FR-426: tax rate "stored as a fraction with six-place adjustment". The rounding rule is not stated.
