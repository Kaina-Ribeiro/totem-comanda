## Notes

### Contradictions and conflicts
- **Pre-orders:** plan decision 17 says no pre-orders in the pilot. Brief rule 4 says scheduled orders are in scope, and the pickup-slot rows (CFG-647 to 667, API-257, API-258) map to `dateToFire` and `pickupTime`. Kai must decide.
- **Tax groups:** the plan cuts "tax groups per service" (CFG-083, 090). I kept per-channel TAX rates in scope, because MODELS 5.16 has them. Confirm this reading.
- **Duplicate labels:** "Enabled" (CFG-049, 052), "Name" (many), "Images" (CFG-154, 174) are the same label on different forms. Each keeps its own id.
- **Duplicate operation names in the API catalogue:** "Shared controls and cross-cutting interactions" (about 10 rows), "Submit validate" (API-311, 312), "Retrieve list" (126, 159, 300), "Retrieve organization" (142, 143, 295), "Retrieve special date" (231, 321). The inputs do not say which is which, so these cannot be mapped one to one.

### Limits the spec admits
- Every API read says "exact response envelope is hidden" and "backend authorization is unknown".
- Async jobs: "job acceptance must not be treated as completed execution" (applies to POS sync and publish).
- External apps are only host contracts: advanced cross-sell editor, receipt QR admin, consumer previews, payment and finance tools, external menu validation, assisted ordering.

### What MODELS.md cannot express (from this chunk)
- Sold out with a duration, and "hidden" as separate from sold out (ITEM has only `available`).
- Special dates (no entity).
- Kiosk attract schedules and guest messages.
- Capacity profiles, order interval, pace, `waitTime`, prep times, item lead time, daily cap.
- Free-delivery threshold, minimum order per channel, delivery zones.
- Section visibility per channel (CATEGORY has none).
- Option `supersede`, SKU, tags (except dietary allergens).
- Cross-sell: the `CROSS_SELLS` enum is defined but not followed (MODELS 5.18).
- Customer note, storefront categories, guest conversion.

### Tables, checks and waiters (rule 3)
- The chunk has no table, check (bill), tab, guest count, service charge or waiter entity.
- Touchpoints: seat orders and their hours, capacity, prices and flags (CFG-063, 074, 081, 082, 095, 120-124, 130, 133, 140, 212, 230, 232, 425, 646).
- Touchpoints: table-bill visibility and consent (CFG-171, 173, 591, 710).
- Touchpoints: StaffFeed, the only waiter-like surface (CFG-010, 726-742; API-017, 018, 019, 060, 061).
- MODELS `IPosCheck` (9.7: `guestCount`, `employeeId`) has no counterpart in this spec.
