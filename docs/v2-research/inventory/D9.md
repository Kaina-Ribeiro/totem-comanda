## Notes

### Contradictions inside the chunk

- **Mislabelled fields.** CFG-210 and CFG-211 are both labelled "SKU" but the effects are "tax group" and "tags". CFG-202 is labelled "Item lead time" but its effect is a suggestive-selling link. CFG-190 is labelled "Calories" but its choices are "Single Size" and "Multiple Sizes". CFG-259 is an unnamed SKU-like field under the title "This will be automatically generated". Confirm the real field names with the spec owner before the editor copies these labels.
- **Two "Item lead time" entries.** CFG-201 (lead time) and CFG-202 (suggestive selling) share a label.
- **Two tag-action name limits.** CFG-279 says max 100 and max 256 in the same entry.
- **Item-flag template defaults.** FEAT-036 says the form shell sets item-menu false, then the new-template setup sets it true. The spec says the later value is effective. Keep one value in the editor.
- **Unlimited maximum.** FEAT-034 says a stored -1 displays as blank, while CFG-236 says unlimited depends on the editor. No single representation is defined.
- **Ids versus UUIDs.** FEAT-043 requires UUID validation for force-push targets. v2 ids are strings such as "1001" (MODELS.md 5.2), so the rule would reject v2 ids.
- **Calories type.** The reference uses a non-negative integer (CFG-191, CFG-246). v2 `calories` is a string ("650").
- **internalName requiredness.** The reference marks internal names required (CFG-198, CFG-240, CFG-251). v2 `internalName` is optional and falls back to `name`.
- **Dine in versus seat.** Dine-in (kiosk, CFG-195, CFG-217, CFG-224) and seat or table (CFG-212, CFG-230, CFG-232, CFG-173) are separate flags. The T column marks only the seat, table and table-bill rows. Dine-in alone may also touch tables. Confirm with Kai.
- **Scheduled orders.** The brief keeps scheduled orders in scope (CFG-201 lead time). plan-next decision 17 says pre-order is not in the pilot.
- **Delivery.** The brief keeps delivery in scope (rule 4). plan-next cuts "delivery and marketplaces" and "tax groups per service". Rows CFG-193, CFG-216 and the TAX link are IN in this inventory and NO in Comanda.
- **Menu import.** The brief does not cut "menu import" by name. plan-next does ("menu import and push"). The CSV rows are marked `?`.

### Unknowns the spec admits

- FEAT-042: the external validator's internal algorithms are Unknown.
- FEAT-034: not every modifier field exists for every subtype; the subtype minimum option count is not defined.
- FEAT-034: nested cloning is not established. Cycle detection is not exhaustive. Default-quantity limits are backend-declared.
- FEAT-038: each health check's rule sits in business-rules.md (BR-200 onward), not in this chunk.
- Referenced but not in this chunk: FORM-025 to FORM-043, PERM-003, PERM-004, PERM-013, PERM-026, FR-154 onward, features.md, business-rules.md, functional-requirements.md.
- Most CFG entries say "No field-level required rule established" and "Loaded value; no verified new-record default". Those defaults are not recorded in the rows except where the spec states one.
