## Notes

### Contradictions inside the chunk

- **Default language fallback:** BR-KIOSK-03 (business-rules.md:36-37) says a missing default language is a maintenance-mode error. The config table (business-rules.md:119) says `en-gb` when absent. These two cannot both hold.
- **Printer check trigger:** features.md:203 says the printer check runs "each time the route CHANGES TO /welcome". BR-KIOSK-18 (business-rules.md:84-85) lists six landing screens (menu, order summary, order type, product search, loading, welcome).
- **Face-ID 2FA attempts:** BR-ALTPAY-09 (business-rules.md:791) says 3 attempts. F-ALTPAY-06 (features.md:1373) says attempts are bounded by the provider session.
- **PE09 retry:** the payment table (business-rules.md:699) says PE09 has no retry. The gift-card PED table (business-rules.md:778) says retry "Try again / other method". The PE01 text also differs between the two tables.
- **Sold-out display:** features.md:521 says unavailability is removal, not greying, and admits no sold-out cell was found. plan-next G-T1 wants greyed "Esgotado". The spec does not support G-T1.

- F-START-02 (features.md:301): per-locale media filter, no call site found.

- F-START-08 (features.md:414): loyalty banner partially confirmed.

### What v2 (MODELS.md) cannot express

- **Kitchen wait time:** `waitTime` and kitchen pace have no v2 field. SubmitOrderResponse has no waitTime.
- **Option rules:** `supersede`, `defaultOption`, role UPSELLS, role REQUIRED-MODIFIER, and composite TOGGLE (positive or negative choice) have no v2 field. COMBO_MAIN exists as an entity type, not as a role value.
- **Display flags:** `showFromPrice`, `showCategoriesPrice`, `displayOrder` (v2 uses list order), `itemLabels`, `customizedItemTags`, `tagsPages`.
- **Discount cap:** `hasReachedMaximumDiscountLimit` and the cap value have no v2 field.
- **Senior discount:** no v2 type for senior or minimum-age discounts. Award types are PSEUDO_CURRENCY, CODE, PROMOTION, ITEM.
- **Cross-sell:** CROSS_SELLS is listed in v2 but its links are not followed (MODELS 5.18).
- **Notification identifier:** buzzer, tent or table number has no field. Only SubmitOrderRequest.notes can carry it.
- **Allergens:** v2 has a fixed enum with no "traces" list and no "não informado" state (plan-next decision 7 needs both).
- **Store status:** maintenance, at-capacity and pace have no v2 flag. Only liveFlag and hours exist.
- **Money units:** the spec uses minor units (BRL limit 1,000,000 = R$ 10,000.00). v2 uses main units, so the limit becomes 10000.00.

### Table, check, waiter and staff content (flag T)

- The spec has **no** table, check, bill, tab, guest count, pay-at-table, request-bill or waiter entity. The only T rows are: eat-in choice (F-START-07), table, tent and buzzer entry (F-POST-02), service charge (F-BASKET-02), help requested and clerk (F-SCO-01), and staff approval (F-BASKET-05 to F-BASKET-09, all CUT).
- v2 IPosCheck (guestCount, employeeId, isOpenedByPosEmployee) and open-check flows have no matching spec behaviour. They need a decision, not a mapping.
