- **Table, check, waiter (T column, 22 rows with Y).** The reference has no table, check, bill, tab, waiter, guest count, pay-at-table or request-bill model. The only touches are: table and tent-card capture (FR-1012, FR-1013, FR-1016, UJ-POST-02, ENT-POST-01); the call-for-staff button (FR-1205 to FR-1208, UJ-SCO-03, DI-A12-SCO-HELP); staff login and clerk actions (ENT-SCO-01, UJ-SCO-02, cut); service charge and itemised fees (FR-411, ENT-BASKET-02, OP-BASKET-01); and the table-number and tent-card notification slots. MODELS.md already has `IPosCheck` (9.7: guestCount, employeeId, openCheck, checkOpenedAtInUTC), `SubmitOrderRequest.openCheck` (8.12), `PayCheckRequest` with `config.tableNumber` (8.14) and `ValidateTableRequest` (8.15). No spec row describes these, so the table, check and waiter behaviour must be specified outside this reference.
- **Contradictions inside the chunk.**
  - Order types: FR-222 and ENT-START-03 allow only DINEIN and TAKE_OUT and filter out DELIVERY, while rule 4 of the brief and MODELS 8.1 put all seven types in scope. Needs Kai's decision.
  - Menu ordering: FR-303 and ENT-MENU-01 to 03 sort by `displayOrder`. MODELS 5.2 rule 4 says list order, and v2 has no `displayOrder` field.
  - Guest name: UJ-E2E-01 step 9 asks for it at the pay gates, FR-1015 and UJ-POST-02 ask for it after the order. The name is cut either way, but the location is inconsistent.
  - Money: FR-420 states the BRL limit as 1,000,000 minor units (R$ 10,000.00). MODELS.md uses decimals in BRL, the brief says decimal, and CLAUDE.md says integer cents. Three conventions in use.
  - Tips: FR-829 to FR-834 and UJ-PAY-03 are in scope by brief rule 4, but CLAUDE.md lists tips as out of scope. The brief is newer.
  - Cut list mismatch: food stamps (FR-807, FR-837), room charge (FR-931 to FR-936, UJ-ALTPAY-04), Face-ID (FR-621 to FR-623, FR-943 to FR-950), and email and SMS receipts (FR-1021 to FR-1023) are cut in plan-next.md or CLAUDE.md, but not in brief rule 5. Marked `?`, not `CUT`.
  - Hidden staff gesture (FR-125, UJ-KIOSK-04) opens a kiosk admin panel. Comanda has a separate back office, so the scope is `?`.
